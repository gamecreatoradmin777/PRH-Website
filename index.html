03 A GOOD PLACE TO PLAY

Good roleplay
starts with respect.

Our guidelines help keep the experience fair, welcoming, and fun for everyone. Take a moment to get familiar before your first shift.

01

In-game regulations

Keep scenes immersive, respect the story, and give every player room to contribute.

+
  1. Follow Roblox Terms of Service: All members must follow Roblox's official Community Standards and Terms of Use.
  2. Maintain professionalism: Staff must use proper grammar, avoid internet slang while on duty, and treat everyone respectfully.
  3. Wear appropriate uniforms: Employees must wear their assigned rank uniform and ID badge while on hospital grounds in-game.
  4. No administrative abuse (AA): Never misuse moderation tools, admin commands, or permissions.
  5. Respect the chain of command: Direct questions or complaints to your immediate supervisor before escalating to high-ranking executives.
  6. No powergaming: Give other players a fair chance to react. Do not declare another character instantly cured or dead without their consent.
  7. No metagaming: Do not use out-of-character information, such as usernames or chat logs, to influence in-game roleplay.
  8. Value your life (FRP): Roleplay realistic fear and physical limitations. Characters cannot survive impossible scenarios.
  9. Use realistic medical procedures: Follow step-by-step hospital protocols in fictional scenes instead of rushing treatment. This is roleplay, not real-world medical guidance.
  10. No ERP or graphic roleplay: Explicit, overly violent, or adult-themed medical scenarios are prohibited.
  11. Stay in your assigned zone: Work only in areas permitted for your rank and department.
  12. Communicate clearly: Use the designated out-of-character brackets, (( like this )), when speaking outside your character.
  13. No tool abuse: Use medical tools, vehicles, and equipment only for official hospital duties. Never use them to troll.
  14. Process patient check-in: Civilians must check in at reception before receiving non-emergency treatment.
  15. Keep areas clear: Do not loiter in or block hallways, doorways, or ambulance bays. Keep emergency routes open.
  16. No free-ranking: Earn promotions through official training, active shifts, or approved applications.
  17. Three-strike policy: A minor hospital-rule violation results in a warning. Three warnings lead to punishment. Continued violations may bring more severe consequences, including demotion, divisional bans, or temporary or permanent community bans.
02

General regulations

Be kind in every channel, follow staff direction, and help us keep this a place to belong.

+
  1. Respectful behavior: Treat everyone respectfully, regardless of beliefs, opinions, or background. Disrespect is not tolerated in any channel.
  2. No harassment: Do not harass, bully, or personally attack any member.
  3. Civility: Keep disagreements respectful and constructive. Avoid discussing politics.
  4. No hate speech: Hate speech, discrimination, and offensive language targeting people or groups based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, or similar traits are not tolerated.
  5. English only: Use English in all text and voice channels to keep communication clear and consistent.
  6. No trolling or disruptive behavior: Do not disrupt conversations, instigate drama, cause arguments, or disrupt voice channels.
  7. No spam or self-promotion: Do not spam, advertise, or excessively self-promote. Avoid word walls and single-word message spam. Advertising requires approval.
  8. Respect privacy: Do not share another person's private information without their consent.
  9. Post mindfully: Do not post explicit or NSFW content, gore, or images of that nature. Avoid excessive swearing and aggressive language.
  10. Use the right channels: Post in channels that match your message. Share GIFs and images in media channels, and do not spam.
  11. Use mentions appropriately: Do not ping high-ranking members without a valid question or inquiry.
  12. One account per person: Alternate accounts are not allowed in the server.
  13. Match your account nickname: Your display name must match your Roblox name. Verify through Bloxlink to set it.
  14. Follow Discord's Terms of Service: Follow Discord's Terms of Service and guidelines. Moderation may extend beyond removal from the Discord server.
  15. No defamation: Defamation of PRH or any part of the community is not tolerated and may result in severe moderation action.
  16. Report concerns: Promptly report concerning behavior to a moderator or administrator.
  17. Follow moderator guidance: Follow moderator and administrator instructions; their server decisions are final. You may appeal a moderation action by opening a ticket or submitting a ban appeal.

These rules govern PRH roleplay and community spaces. Medical procedures described here are fictional roleplay only, not real-world medical advice.
